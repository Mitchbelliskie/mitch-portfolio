CCTV Installation

PROJECT STATUS: Learning / Practical Reference

This project covers CCTV system planning, camera placement, recording equipment setup and basic fault diagnosis.

Main Components

  • CCTV cameras
  • DVR or NVR recorder
  • Suitable power supply or PoE switch
  • Ethernet or approved video cables
  • Storage drive and monitor
  • Network router, where remote access is required

Installation Workflow

  • Confirm the owner's requirements and camera coverage areas.
  • Plan camera positions, cable routes and power requirements.
  • Mount cameras securely with suitable weather protection where needed.
  • Connect cameras to the recorder or network using compatible equipment.
  • Configure date, time, recording schedules and authorised user access.
  • Test image quality, night vision, recording and playback.
  • Document camera locations and system settings securely.

Common Faults

  • No image: Check power, cable connections and camera status.
  • Blurry image: Inspect the lens, focus and protective cover.
  • No recording: Check storage status, recording schedule and system time.
  • Night image problems: Check infrared illumination and nearby reflections.
  • Remote access failure: Check network connectivity and authorised access settings.

Maintenance and Security

  • Clean camera lenses and inspect mounts and cables.
  • Check recording and playback regularly.
  • Update firmware and use strong, unique passwords.
  • Restrict access to authorised users and protect recorded footage.
  • Respect privacy and applicable laws when positioning cameras.
