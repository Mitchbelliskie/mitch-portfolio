Electrical Wiring & Installation

PROJECT STATUS: Learning / Practical Reference

This project covers the principles of electrical wiring, installation planning, circuit protection and safe inspection practices.

Key Areas

  • Understanding wiring diagrams and circuit layouts
  • Identifying cables, switches, sockets and protective devices
  • Understanding earthing and bonding
  • Learning the purpose of fuses, circuit breakers and RCDs
  • Organising wiring neatly and labelling circuits

Installation Process

  • Review the requirements and applicable wiring standards.
  • Plan circuit routes and suitable cable and protection ratings.
  • Isolate the supply and verify that the circuit is de-energised before work.
  • Have wiring installed and inspected by a qualified electrician.
  • Perform required continuity, insulation-resistance, polarity and protective-device tests.
  • Record test results and label the installation.

Common Problems

  • Tripping breaker: Possible overload, short circuit or earth fault.
  • Loose connection: Can cause heating, intermittent operation or arcing.
  • Damaged insulation: May cause leakage current or electric shock.
  • Missing or faulty earthing: Can make exposed metal parts dangerous.

Safety

Electrical installations can cause fatal shock and fires. Do not work on live wiring. Installation, fault-finding and electrical testing should be carried out by a qualified person using appropriate test equipment and local standards.
