Medical Equipment Electrical Safety Testing

PROJECT STATUS: Learning / Practical Reference

Electrical safety testing helps assess whether medical equipment presents unacceptable electrical hazards to patients, operators or service personnel.

Purpose

  • Identify damaged cables, plugs and enclosures.
  • Assess protective earth continuity where applicable.
  • Measure insulation resistance when permitted by the service procedure.
  • Measure equipment leakage current using an appropriate safety analyser.
  • Document results against the applicable standard and manufacturer limits.

Equipment Used

  • Electrical safety analyser
  • Digital multimeter for suitable preliminary checks
  • Inspection tools and approved test leads
  • Manufacturer's service manual and test records

General Test Workflow

  • Identify the equipment, model and applicable test requirements.
  • Inspect the casing, power cord, plug and accessories.
  • Remove the equipment from clinical use if damage or a safety concern is found.
  • Perform required tests with a suitable analyser, following the manual and applicable standard.
  • Compare results with the correct limits for that equipment and applied part classification.
  • Record readings, instrument details, outcome and any corrective action.

Important Precautions

Incorrect testing can damage equipment or create a shock hazard. Only trained personnel should conduct these tests. Follow the equipment manufacturer's instructions and applicable standards, and never return equipment to clinical use until required checks are passed.

Documentation

A useful record includes equipment identification, test date, analyser identification, test results, acceptance limits, pass/fail outcome, technician and follow-up action.
