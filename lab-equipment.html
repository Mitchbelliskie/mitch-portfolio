Laboratory Equipment Maintenance

PROJECT STATUS: Learning / Practical Reference

Routine inspection and maintenance help laboratory equipment operate reliably and produce dependable results.

Equipment Covered

  • Laboratory centrifuges
  • Water baths and incubators
  • Autoclaves and sterilizers
  • pH meters
  • Water distillers and deionizers
  • Laboratory refrigerators

Maintenance Workflow

  • Identify the equipment and review its service manual.
  • Inspect the casing, power cord, plug and accessories.
  • Clean the equipment using the manufacturer's approved method.
  • Check controls, indicators, alarms and safety features.
  • Carry out scheduled functional tests and calibration where required.
  • Record inspection findings, readings, faults and corrective actions.

Common Faults

  • Centrifuge vibration: Check rotor condition, balance, loading and mounting according to the manual.
  • Incubator temperature error: Check settings, door closure, sensor readings and airflow.
  • Water bath not heating: Check water level, settings and reported error codes.
  • Unstable pH readings: Check electrode condition, calibration buffers and measurement procedure.
  • Refrigerator temperature issue: Check door seals, airflow, settings and temperature records.

Documentation

Maintenance records should include equipment identification, service date, inspection results, test readings, parts replaced, technician details and recommended follow-up.

Safety

Follow infection-control procedures and equipment-specific instructions. Isolate power before servicing where appropriate. Never bypass interlocks or operate damaged equipment. Sterilizers, centrifuges and other hazardous equipment require trained personnel and approved procedures.
